TAAL The architecture of Rhythm Exploring the anatomy of Taal—the heartbeat of Indian classical music, and how its rhythm guides Kathak dancers and Tabla players to move, listen, and perform in harmony. LET’S EXPLORE 5 TYPES OF ‘TAAL’ - Teen Taal - Dadra Taal - Kaharwa Taal - Jhap Taal - Ek Taal We will be showing it in X1 Ek Gun, X2 Do Gun and X4 Chau Gun Speeds. This with help us understand the 'Bol' along with the Foot movements of each Taal.
